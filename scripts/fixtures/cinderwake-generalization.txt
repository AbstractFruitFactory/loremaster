Test Campaign Session Transcript — Cinderwake: The Blue Key

DM: Three months after the East Sluice collapsed, you are back in Cinderwake looking for Tessa Vale, the cartographer who vanished two years ago.

Mira: We start at the Chapel of Tides. Sister Vale was supposed to know Tessa, right?

DM: Sister Vale knew her from the relief kitchens after the Ashfall fire.

Jon: Is Sister Vale related to Tessa or Corin Vale?

DM: Not that anyone here knows. Vale is a common harbor surname.

Mira: Good, because I was already building a conspiracy board.

---

DM: Sister Vale gives you a sealed note Tessa left with her two years ago. She says Tessa told her to give it to anyone asking about the old survey tunnels.

Jon: I open it.

DM: It reads: “If you find my blue-glass key, do not take it to the Beacon. Alda is wrong. It opens the maintenance lift beneath the Salt Archive.”

Mira: Who is Alda?

DM: Alda Quinn, the harbor engineer. She still oversees the eastern works.

Jon: So Tessa thought Alda was wrong about the key.

DM: Correct.

---

DM: On the way to the Salt Archive, you pass the ferry stairs and Orren Pike waves you down.

Mira: The ferryman?

DM: Yes. He says he heard you were asking about Tessa.

Jon: “Did you see her before she vanished?”

DM: Orren says, “I saw her at the Old Beacon the night before she disappeared. She was arguing with someone in a gray coat.”

Mira: Did he see the other person’s face?

DM: No. Orren says the fog was too thick.

Jon: Is anyone else known to have seen this?

DM: Not that you know of.

---

DM: You reach the Salt Archive. The public reading room is open, but the basement is restricted.

Mira: We ask for Maelin Sorn.

DM: Brother Maelin is the archive keeper. He lets you into Tessa’s old workroom because Sister Vale’s note has her seal.

Jon: Search everything.

DM: In a locked drawer you find a blue-glass key wrapped in oilcloth, a brass customs key, and a small notebook.

Mira: Three separate things?

DM: Yes. Blue-glass key, brass customs key, notebook.

Jon: I want to know what the brass key opens.

DM: Maelin recognizes it as an old customs strongroom key.

Mira: Recognition, not proof.

DM: Fair.

---

DM: The notebook contains survey sketches of the East Sluice, the Old Beacon, and the Salt Archive. One margin note says: “Alda keeps insisting the blue key belongs to the Beacon. It does not.”

Jon: Anything about the sluice collapse?

DM: There is an entry from four months ago: “Retaining bolts on gate three show fresh lateral scoring. If they fail, it will look like corrosion.”

Mira: The collapse was three months ago, right?

DM: Correct.

Jon: So Tessa noticed something a month before it collapsed.

DM: That timing is correct.

---

DM: You find Alda Quinn at the East Sluice works.

Mira: I show her the blue key and ask what it opens.

DM: Alda says, “Old Beacon service door. Tessa knew that.”

Jon: We do not tell her about the note.

Mira: I ask why the sluice collapsed.

DM: Alda says the retaining bolts were forty years old and finally gave out.

Jon: Can we inspect them?

DM: Yes. The failed bolts are still stored in an evidence crate.

Mira: Engineering check, 21.

DM: The metal around the breaks is not pitted like corrosion. Several bolts have parallel tool marks and clean cuts from the inside face. Someone deliberately weakened them before the collapse.

Jon: Can we tell who?

DM: No.

---

DM: At the customs annex, the brass key opens the old records strongroom.

Mira: So that one is confirmed.

DM: Yes. Inside you find tax rolls and a red leather ledger from eleven years ago, the year of the Ashfall fire.

Jon: Anything juicy?

DM: One page lists irregular payments made by House Sorn. The entries use initials. One reads: “C.V. — eighty crowns — ash disposal.”

Mira: Corin Vale?

DM: You do not know who C.V. is.

Jon: Does it say anything about starting the fire?

DM: No. The ledger does not say that.

Mira: Could “ash disposal” just be cleanup?

DM: Could be. The ledger does not explain it.

---

DM: Maelin later tells you the Ashfall fire destroyed the old Ember Market eleven years ago. Corin Vale was dockmaster then and is still dockmaster now.

Jon: Tessa’s father, right?

DM: Yes. Corin Vale is Tessa Vale’s father.

Mira: Does Maelin think C.V. means Corin Vale?

DM: He says, “Maybe. I would not swear to it.”

---

DM: You take the blue-glass key to the Salt Archive basement. Behind a shelving rack is a narrow iron panel with a blue-glass keyway.

Jon: Moment of truth.

DM: The key fits and opens a maintenance lift descending beneath the archive.

Mira: So Tessa’s note was right about where it fits.

DM: Yes.

---

DM: In the lower maintenance level you find old engineering plans for something called the Tide Engine.

Jon: The thing sailors blame for Founder’s Tide?

DM: Maelin says sailors have told that story for decades: the Tide Engine caused Founder’s Tide forty-two years ago.

Mira: Do the plans prove it?

DM: No. A maintenance log says pressure in the engine rose sharply shortly before the flood, but the cause of the flood is listed as undetermined.

Jon: So correlation, not proof.

DM: Correct.

---

DM: The final page of Tessa’s notebook says: “If I disappear, do not assume Alda killed me. She is hiding something about the sluice, but I have no proof she means me harm.”

Mira: Extremely considerate of her to constrain our accusations.

DM: And that is where we end.
